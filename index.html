About me

Hi, I'm Pau

I'm a gameplay programmer from Barcelona with a degree in Interactive Digital Content from ENTI, Universitat de Barcelona.

Most of my work sits where programming meets design. I like building tools that let designers iterate without waiting on code, and then spending time on the numbers: damage, health, waves and difficulty curves.

During my internship at Ubisoft Mobile Barcelona I worked in QC, validating game balance against design targets and documenting bugs in Jira. It taught me how a production team ships, and how much clear reporting matters.

I'm a collaborative teammate and an active listener, and I adapt quickly when plans change.
